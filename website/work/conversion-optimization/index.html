B2B technology services: the business behind the work

An agency website supports a considered purchase. Readers need a path from educational content to service evaluation, contact and a sales conversation.

How the work connects: This is the conversion layer of the Golden Owl demand programme. Acquisition brings visitors; the journey and sales handoff determine whether they become qualified opportunities.

Golden Owl acquisitionGolden Owl lead qualityTheLook e-commerce

The challenge

A large blog audience was not reaching the pages that help prospects evaluate services and make contact.

How I delivered the work

  • Combined GA4 page paths, Hotjar click and scroll maps and HubSpot lead context.
  • Identified weak routes from articles to service pages, heavily clicked service tiles without links and forms abandoned after partial completion.
  • Specified contextual article CTAs, simpler forms, clearer service navigation and stronger portfolio proof.
  • Translated recommendations into CMS requirements so the content team could test copy and visuals.

What the work showed

Funnel gap

In the January–July 2025 source analysis, 94.8% of organic sessions landed on blog content. Only about 0.10% of blog sessions continued to service pages.

Observed improvement

The existing case reports lead conversion moving from approximately 0.6% to 2.1% after the wider roadmap and concurrent marketing activity.

Actionable UX evidence

Heatmaps revealed dead clicks and useful content placed below the depth many visitors reached.

The marketing decision

Where this leads

Create clear routes from relevant content into services, reduce contact friction, and test the journey step by step with a consistent lead definition.
