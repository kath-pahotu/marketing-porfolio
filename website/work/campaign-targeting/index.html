Banking and campaign efficiency: the business behind the work

A bank’s outbound campaign has limited call capacity. The business choice is which customers to prioritize while protecting reach and customer experience.

How the work connects: Independent propensity analysis supports audience planning. It complements campaign execution but needs a prospective pilot before claiming live improvement.

The challenge

Identify which customers to contact first without using information only available after the contact.

How I delivered the work

  • Built and compared pre-contact models using 45,211 historical contacts.
  • Separated training and test data and excluded post-contact information.
  • Compared response capture at different campaign capacities and specified pilot measures.

What the work showed

Offline targeting

On the untouched test set, the top 20% of ranked contacts captured 51.8% of historical responders.

Response efficiency

That band converted at 30.3%, a 2.59× lift versus the historical baseline; it represents 80% fewer contacts in an offline scenario.

The marketing decision

Where this leads

Pilot the prioritized band against the existing targeting approach and measure incremental conversions, contact cost, customer complaints and opt-outs.

Project evidence

Context and sources

This UCI dataset analysis is an independent project. Historical predictive lift is not causal campaign lift, and the contact reduction was not implemented. Production use needs appropriate feature review, customer identifiers and time-based validation.
