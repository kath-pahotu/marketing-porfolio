Experimentation · Rokt holdout experiment

Did the ads produce additional conversions?

A positive conversion signal, evaluated against a suppressed-ad holdout and checked before using it to size a campaign rollout.

The decision

Did ad exposure increase conversion, and how much does that conclusion depend on the unit used to count users?

My contribution: I framed the question, prepared and validated the data, performed the analysis, built the reporting output and wrote the recommendations.

How I approached it

Data cleaning

Removed 36,893 exact duplicates, excluded an invalid cohort row and normalized device values, leaving 852,197 cleaned records.

Experiment evaluation

Compared unique converted users with unique users within each group, using a two-proportion z-test and confidence intervals.

Assignment-unit audit

Found 26,426 users appearing in both groups across months and 92 overlapping user-cohort pairs. Monthly reassignment is plausible, but the randomization unit is not explicitly documented.

Sensitivity analysis

Re-estimated the comparison at user-cohort and session level. Used the difference to qualify the headline effect and define the evidence needed for rollout.

Results and interpretation

2.23%treatment conversion
1.95%holdout conversion
+13.9%primary relative uplift; p = 3.42e-09
+5.5%user-cohort uplift; p = 0.016

The primary comparison suggests a positive effect, with a relative-uplift interval of +9.1% to +19.0%. The user-cohort sensitivity estimate is smaller. The direction is encouraging; the magnitude depends on how assignment and repeat users are handled.

What I recommended

Confirm the randomization unit and reassignment policy before sizing rollout. Investigate the decline across monthly cohorts and use device differences to propose follow-up tests.

What the evidence does not establish

This is analysis of a supplied dataset, not a campaign I ran for Rokt. Repeated users can create dependence across months. Conventional p-values do not resolve the assignment ambiguity. The estimated additional conversions are a counterfactual calculation, not verified realized impact.
