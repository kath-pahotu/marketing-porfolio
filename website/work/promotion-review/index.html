Promotion efficiency · ZaloPay promotion review

Focus promotion review where budget exposure concentrates

Connect promotion-cost concentration with investigation capacity while preserving the distinction between unusual behavior and confirmed abuse.

The decision

Which users deserve manual review, and what share of credited promotion cost is associated with a manageable review queue?

My contribution: I framed the question, prepared and validated the data, performed the analysis, built the reporting output and wrote the recommendations.

How I approached it

SQL exploration and features

Examined campaign performance and built behavioral signals for immediate redemption, credited discount, referrals, shared devices, shared IPs and reciprocal transfers.

Cost concentration

Compared the score-based population with its share of credited promotion cost, preserving the distinction between scored users and all campaign users.

Python rule simulation

Simulated alternative rules and reconciled their outputs with SQL. Compared queue size and cost exposure rather than claiming unobserved fraud precision.

Decision reporting

Built Power BI views for budget concentration, merchant exposure, review priority, rule trade-offs and overall-payment retention context.

Results and interpretation

86,170scored users
4.26%score-based priority cohort
26.94%credited cost associated with that cohort
1,994users in the balanced candidate queue

The score-based cohort contains 3,671 users. Separately, the medium-balanced candidate flags 1,994 users (2.31% of scored users) and covers 23.89% of credited promotion cost. These are different definitions, so their percentages should not be combined.

What I recommended

Start the balanced rule in shadow mode with manual review. Collect investigator outcomes to evaluate customer impact and precision before considering automated actions.

What the evidence does not establish

There are no confirmed fraud labels. A flag is a review-priority signal, not proof of fraud; cost exposure is not recovered savings. Zero low-score impact is an internal consistency check, not a measured false-positive rate. Overall-payment retention is not campaign-attributed retention. This was not employment at ZaloPay.
