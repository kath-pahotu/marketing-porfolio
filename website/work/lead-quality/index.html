Business objective: Give marketing and sales a consistent view of lead quality, source performance and sales progression.

The challengeWhat I didWhat I learnedMarketing decisionProject evidenceContext & sources

The challenge

Lead volume alone could not explain which enquiries were useful or whether follow-up was working.

How I delivered the work

  • Connected CRM lead records to acquisition source and grouped outcomes by country and sales owner.
  • Documented the qualified-lead stage and reporting definitions.
  • Designed a recurring review surface for marketing, sales and business development.

What the work showed

Source quality

The view made differences between high-volume and high-quality sources visible.

Market fit

Country-level outcomes helped identify enquiries that were poorly matched to the service offer.

Shared accountability

Sales-owner cuts helped separate lead-quality questions from follow-up questions.

The marketing decision

Where this leads

Allocate attention using qualified outcomes, agree one lead definition, and discuss acquisition and follow-up in the same review.
