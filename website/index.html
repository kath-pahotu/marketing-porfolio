ZaloPay studyIndependent promotion analysis

Where does promotion cost concentrate?

A small share of scored users was associated with a much larger share of credited promotion cost.

The next marketing move

Start with manual review, collect outcomes and check customer harm before automating any action.

Both shares describe the same score-based group. Flags are review signals, not confirmed fraud or recovered savings.

Mobility & deliveryIndependent confidential case · Public summary

Is growth supported by repeat use?

Partner scale, customer return and subsidy intensity need to be read together to assess growth.

The next marketing move

Test targeted incentives with a holdout and evaluate activated, retained users alongside cost.

This public view is qualitative and sanitized. Proposed tests and forecasts are not realized results.

TheLook ecommerceIndependent study · Synthetic data

What follows the first purchase?

Customer groups turn a broad retention question into distinct priorities for relationship-building and reactivation.

The next marketing move

Design different treatments for valuable repeat, developing and lapsed customers; evaluate tests against net value.

Synthetic-data analysis, not client campaign results. The study does not establish ROAS or causal uplift.

Did the advertising add conversions?
