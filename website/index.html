  1. Independent targeting study

    Bank audience prioritization

    A pre-contact model turns historical response patterns into a practical proposal for a limited campaign pilot.

    Methods & evidence for Bank audience prioritization

    SQL, Python, scikit-learn

    Model comparison and capacity evaluation: the top 20% of the historical contact list captured 51.8% of responders. The recommendation is a controlled campaign pilot.

    Offline predictive performance, not implemented campaign uplift.

  2. Independent promotion study

    ZaloPay promotion-cost review

    Promotion behaviour and cost exposure are connected with the team’s review capacity and customer-impact safeguards.

    Methods & evidence for ZaloPay promotion-cost review

    T-SQL, Python, Power BI

    User-level reconciliation and explainable rules balance credited-cost coverage, review workload and customer-impact guardrails.

    Review signals are not confirmed fraud, and credited-cost exposure is not recovered savings.
